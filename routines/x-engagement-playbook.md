# X Engagement Playbook (manual, ~15–20 min/day)

The research is clear: for an account starting near zero (~360 followers),
original tweets alone go nowhere. Roughly 70% of small-account growth on X
comes from substantive replies to larger in-niche accounts, posted soon
after their tweets go up. This is the part no routine can automate — X is
login-gated to automation, and AI-sounding replies are algorithmically
down-ranked and culturally mocked. It has to be you, in your own words.

## Daily play (15–20 minutes, ideally morning IST + one US-morning check)

1. Open X, scan the target list below (make it an X List so it's one click).
2. Find 2–4 fresh tweets (under ~2 hours old) where you have real
   practitioner experience to add.
3. Reply with substance: one specific thing you've seen, tried, or
   disagree with from experience. 1–3 sentences. Never "Great post!",
   never a restatement of their tweet, never a link to your own content.
4. Post your own daily tweet (from the weekly drafts) when you can stay
   online ~30 minutes to answer replies. Reply to every reply you get,
   fastest in the first hour.
5. 0–2 hashtags ever. Stay in the lane (AI x engineering management) —
   the algorithm learns your topic from who engages.

## Getting reply help from Claude

The weekly Twitter routine includes a best-effort "reply opportunities"
step, but the sandbox usually cannot see X. The reliable path: when you
see a tweet worth replying to, paste its text into any Claude session and
ask for 2–3 reply options in your voice — then edit before posting so it
is genuinely yours. Never post a reply you didn't rewrite at least a little.
